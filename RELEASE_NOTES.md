☰ Menu → **Updates** lists everything with an update in one place (Deckhand and what it installed for you): pick Deckhand to update it, pick anything else to open it at its Update button, or check again. Under Installed, a program can now be given its own **Proton version**. Uninstalling Deckhand while Steam is open no longer leaves its shortcut behind for good: Deckhand leaves the app menu at once, and its shortcut and the rest are removed by themselves the next time Steam is closed. Long menus fit a scaled desktop (they take as many columns as the height needs). A GE-Proton update reads "GE-Proton10-30" instead of repeating the name.
